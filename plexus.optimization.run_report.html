plexus.optimization.run_report module

Durable, append-only reporting for one periodic optimization run.

The service deliberately owns no optimization orchestration. Callers execute the decision stages with persist=False and publish their returned packets at well-defined milestones. A single Task is the lifecycle authority; one Report is its stable stakeholder location; ReportBlocks contain only compact pointers to immutable JSON/XLSX revisions.
