Subject: SRx/HCS Information Accuracy updates

Hi Liz,

We reviewed the recent SRx/HCS feedback and updated the Information Accuracy component scores tied to the issues you raised. I’ve attached the updated guidelines for each component score so your team can review the exact wording being applied.

Summary of score updates:

- Information Accuracy: False Service Claims
  - Updated to allow FAQ-approved proactive delivery language, including statements that SelectRx uses a proactive delivery model or that the box should arrive before the customer needs to start taking the medication.
  - Updated to continue flagging that language when it becomes a specific accelerated delivery promise, such as guaranteed next-day or overnight medication delivery.
  - Updated to flag unqualified next-day or overnight delivery statements unless the agent clearly explains it is optional expedited shipping with an additional customer-paid cost.
  - Updated to flag explicit free/no-cost/cost-free SelectRx program or service claims.
  - Updated to flag statements that the customer is entitled to SelectRx because of membership, a plan, or similar entitlement wording.
  - Updated to flag trial-style language such as “try it out and see how you feel.”
  - Updated to preserve the correction that enrollment/setup may take about 5-10 minutes when the timing refers to enrollment, not medication delivery.

- Information Accuracy: Ambiguous Cost Language
  - Updated to flag answers that tell the customer there is “no cost,” “no cost to use the service,” or similar language in response to a cost question unless the agent also clearly explains that medication copays or member responsibility may still apply.
  - Updated to flag billing language that implies the customer does not need to pay a bill, can skip payment if unable to pay, or can pay whenever with no due-date expectation.
  - Updated to flag free/included next-day or overnight delivery language when the agent does not explain the additional customer-paid expedited shipping cost.

- Information Accuracy: False Local Pharmacy Claims
  - Updated to allow short-term bridge language, including instructions that the customer may need to pick up prescriptions within the next 5 days, in the next few days, or within about a week while SelectRx synchronizes medications.
  - Updated to flag statements that the customer should use a local pharmacy until the first SelectRx delivery, first box, or first shipment arrives.
  - Updated to flag wording that makes the local pharmacy and SelectRx sound interchangeable for the same selected medications.
  - Updated so a later compliant “within 5 days” recap does not erase an earlier noncompliant local-pharmacy statement.

- Information Accuracy: False Carrier Claims
  - Updated to flag statements that make SelectRx sound like it is provided through, tailored through, or available because of the customer’s carrier or plan.
  - Updated to flag statements that there is no delivery fee or service fee because of the customer’s plan.
  - Updated to flag statements that the customer would be missing out on a plan benefit if they do not enroll.
  - This score still allows normal insurance language when the agent is only explaining copays, coverage, network, or patient responsibility.

- Information Accuracy: Copay Guarantees
  - Updated to flag unsupported claims that copays will stay the same, should be the same, will not exist, or will only be a small amount.
  - Updated to flag unsupported statements that the customer has a fixed period, such as 30 days, to pay copays.
  - Updated to flag unsupported coupon or hardship/payment relief statements.
  - This score still allows agents to explain that copays and medication costs are determined by the customer’s insurance plan or network.

- Information Accuracy: Savings Promises
  - Reviewed during this pass, but no behavior change was needed based on the direct component feedback we found.
  - This score continues to flag unsupported promises that SelectRx will save the customer money, lower copays, make medications free, price match, or cover medication costs.
  - It continues to allow approved HCS Discount Rx Card language when it is not presented as a SelectRx savings promise.

We also intentionally kept these as component-score updates. The Information Accuracy composite score was not part of this update pass.
